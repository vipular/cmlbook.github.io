# Gaussian Mixture Models

(1.GMM) Consider a GMM with two Gaussian components (numbered 0 and 1). The 1D-sample points are given as [-2.5, -2, -0.5, 1, 1.5, 3], and their corresponding initial Gaussian assignments are [0, 0, 0, 0, 1, 1]. Perform two iterations of the Expectation-Maximization (EM) algorithm and determine the $\mu$, $\sigma$, and $\pi$'s of the GMM components.
