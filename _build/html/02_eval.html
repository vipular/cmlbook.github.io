\(p(x)=[0.1,0.1,0.8]\) for \(x=0,1,2\)

\(q(x)=[0.8,0.1,0.1]\) for \(x=0,1,2\). Find the KL-divergence \(K L[p \| q]\) rounded off to the nearest integer. (Hint: \(K L[p \| q]=-\sum_{x} p(x) \log \left(\frac{q(x)}{p(x)}\right)\) )

  • -1

  • 1

  • -2

  • 2
