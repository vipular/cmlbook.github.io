# Probability Theory

1. (PrT) A certain COVID test has a true positive rate, P(detected|covid), of 90% and a false negative rate, 1-P(not detected|noCovid), of 5%. In my city, 1% population currently has COVID. I took the test and it came out to be positive. What is the probability that I actually have COVID?
      - $>$ 90%
      - 75% - 90%
      - 50% - 75%
      - 25% - 50%
      - 10% - 25%
      - $<$ 10%

2. (PrT) A certain pdf $p(x)$ is sampled 10 times and the samples come out to be $[4.0, 2.5, 3.2, 5.3, 4.6, 3.8, 2.6, 2.8, 3.7, 4.2]$. Estimate $\int xp(x)dx$.

3. (PrT) A random variable $x$ follows a Gaussian distribution with mean $1$ and variance $4$. What is $\mathbb{E}[x^2]$?
   - 1
   - 2
   - 3
   - 4
   - 5

4. (PrT) Given the data as a string "$aabbbbcacccbbaaccca$", what is :
   - $p(a)$, i.e., probability of a randomly selected character being $a$
   - $p(a|c)$, i.e., probability of a character being $a$, given that the character preceding it in the string is $c$

5. (PrT) Suppose that we have three coloured boxes r (red), b (blue), and g (green). Box r contains 3 apples, 4 oranges, and 3 limes, box b contains 1 apple, 1 orange, and 0 limes, and box g contains 3 apples, 3 oranges, and 4 limes. If a box is chosen at random with probabilities p(r) = 0.2, p(b) = 0.2, p(g) = 0.6, and a piece of fruit is removed from the box (with equal probability of selecting any of the items in the box), then what is the probability of selecting an apple? If we observe that the selected fruit is in fact an orange, what is the probability that it came from the green box? (PRML problem 1.3)

6. (PrT) Show that if two variables x and y are independent, then their covariance is
zero. (PRML problem 1.6)
